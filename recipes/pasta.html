Pasta

Description

A quick and delicious pasta recipe with garlic, olive oil, and Parmesan cheese.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti pasta
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic cloves
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Salt & pepper

Steps

  1. Boil pasta until al dente.
  2. Sauté garlic in olive oil.
  3. Toss pasta with garlic oil.
  4. Sprinkle with Parmesan before serving.
