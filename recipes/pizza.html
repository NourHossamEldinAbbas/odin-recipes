Pizza

Description

A simple homemade pizza recipe with a crispy crust, rich tomato sauce, and gooey mozzarella cheese.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni (optional)
  • Olive oil & herbs

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 220°C (425°F).
  2. Spread tomato sauce over dough.
  3. Top with mozzarella and toppings.
  4. Bake for 15 minutes until golden and bubbly.
