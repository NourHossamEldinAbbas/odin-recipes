Lasagna

This is a delicious lasagna recipe that’s perfect for family dinners!

From Wikipedia

Description

This lasagna is a hearty, cheesy, and flavorful Italian dish made with layers of pasta, savory meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and gooey mozzarella cheese.

Perfect for family dinners or special gatherings, this recipe is a classic comfort food that everyone will love.

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 jar tomato sauce
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • Salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning to taste
  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  3. Brown the ground beef in a skillet, then add tomato sauce and seasonings.
  4. In a bowl, mix ricotta cheese, egg, Parmesan, salt, and pepper.
  5. In a baking dish, layer noodles, meat sauce, ricotta mixture, and mozzarella. Repeat layers.
  6. Top with remaining mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  7. Bake for 45 minutes, then let cool for 10 minutes before serving.
